# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small-to-mid teams running projects: leads planning sprints and tracking delivery, members working through their own tasks. They live in the dashboard daily. The landing page's job is different: a team lead evaluating TaskFlow should understand it and trust it in under a minute.

## Product Purpose

TaskFlow is a project management platform for high-performing teams: plan, track, and deliver projects with clarity and speed. Success looks like a team adopting it for daily planning and status without needing training.

## Brand Personality

Calm, confident, precise. Quiet authority: the interface should feel like it already has things under control. Clarity over spectacle, generous whitespace, restrained color anchored by the violet primary.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS template: gradient blobs, identical feature-card grids, hero-metric clichés.
- Corporate enterprise: navy-and-gray, stock photography of meetings.
- Bubbly consumer app: mascots, illustration-heavy layouts, excessive rounding.
- Dark techy/crypto: neon accents on black, glassmorphism, glow effects.

## Design Principles

- Calm is the feature: the product organizes chaos, so the interface never adds any.
- One accent, used with intent: violet marks the primary action, never the decoration.
- Type carries the hierarchy: scale and weight before boxes and borders.
- The landing should feel like the app: the marketing proves the product's clarity by having it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: 4.5:1 text contrast, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, semantic landmarks and keyboard navigation.
